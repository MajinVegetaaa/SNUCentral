# Abstract

<!-- IEEE abstract: one paragraph, no citations, no figures; ~230 words. Index terms are in 00_title.md. -->

University students often struggle to find answers to policy questions because the relevant rules are scattered across long PDF documents on several platforms and are written in formal language. We present **SNU Central**, a retrieval-augmented question-answering system that answers questions about Shiv Nadar University's policies using only official documents, and links every claim to the document, page, section and paragraph it came from. The system indexes 39 public policy documents (660 pages, 2,801 passages) with an inverted index, TF-IDF and BM25 ranking, synonym expansion and spelling correction, all implemented from scratch. An open-weight language model, `gpt-oss-120b`, phrases answers solely from the retrieved passages; it may cite only passage numbers, which the application validates and converts into citations from metadata. A length-normalised BM25 confidence gate declines clearly off-topic questions before the model is called. On a hand-labelled test set of 25 questions, BM25 outperformed TF-IDF (MRR 0.792 vs. 0.581; F1@5 0.609 vs. 0.453). The system declined all five unanswerable questions without refusing any answerable one, and 95% of its answers were factually correct. At the statement level, 94% of answer statements were supported by a cited passage, and every cited statement was supported by its citation, whereas an audit of commercial generative search engines, in a broader open-web setting, found 51.5% of sentences supported. Spelling correction recovered the full retrieval loss caused by typos. The system runs on an 8 GB laptop at no cost and answers in under two seconds.
